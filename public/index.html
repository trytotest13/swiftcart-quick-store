About Us

About SwiftCart

Everything, delivered fast.

SwiftCart is a quick commerce platform built to get groceries, gadgets, and everyday essentials from nearby stores to your door in minutes, without the usual back-and-forth of traditional online shopping.

What SwiftCart is

SwiftCart brings together grocery, electronics, home, fashion, and beauty products in one app, backed by a delivery network built for speed. Instead of browsing a warehouse-sized catalog and waiting days for a parcel, you get a curated selection of everyday products with delivery windows measured in minutes.

Our mission

We want ordering something you need to feel as fast and simple as walking to the shop next door, minus the trip. Every part of SwiftCart, from search to checkout to order tracking, is built around that one idea: less waiting, less friction, more time back in your day.

What makes SwiftCart different

Fast delivery Orders are matched to the nearest available store and rider, with live tracking from checkout to doorstep.
Wide product selection Groceries, electronics, home essentials, fashion, and beauty, organized so you can find what you need without digging.
Secure checkout Saved addresses, UPI, and card payments are handled through a checkout flow built around keeping your details protected.
Easy returns Eligible items can be returned in a few taps from your orders page. No call center required.

Customer-first approach

Wallet balances, saved coupons, order history, and delivery tracking are all built into the app so you can manage an order end-to-end without leaving SwiftCart. If something goes wrong, our support team is reachable directly from your profile.

Questions? Reach us any time from your profile menu.

Returns & Refunds

Return Policy

We want you to feel confident ordering on SwiftCart. If something isn't right, most items can be returned quickly from your orders page.

Last updated: 2026

Overview

Returns are handled directly through the SwiftCart app. Eligibility, timing, and the refund method depend on the type of product ordered, as outlined below.

Eligible & non-returnable products

Generally eligible Electronics, home, fashion, and beauty items in unused, resellable condition with original packaging.
Non-returnable Perishable groceries, fresh produce, and items marked as non-returnable on the product page, for hygiene and safety reasons.

Return window

Eligible non-grocery items can be returned within the window shown on the order, visible under Your orders for each delivered item. Grocery orders are covered by our damaged/incorrect item policy below rather than a standard return window.

How to request a return

Open your orders Go to Profile → Your orders and select the delivered item you'd like to return.
Choose a reason Select the return reason so we can route it correctly and speed up your refund.
Track the pickup or drop-off Follow the on-screen instructions for pickup scheduling or drop-off, where applicable.

Product condition requirements

  • Item should be unused and in its original condition.
  • Original packaging, tags, and accessories should be included where applicable.
  • Proof of purchase is automatically linked through your SwiftCart order history.

Refund process & timing

Once a return is picked up and passes a quick quality check, the refund is issued to your original payment method or SwiftCart Wallet, whichever you select during the return request. Refunds to your SwiftCart Wallet are typically the fastest option; bank and UPI refunds can take a few additional business days to reflect, depending on your bank.

Damaged or incorrect products

If an item arrives damaged, incorrect, or missing (including grocery orders), report it from Your orders within 24 hours of delivery for the fastest resolution.

Contact support

For anything not covered here, reach our support team from your profile menu and we'll help sort it out.
